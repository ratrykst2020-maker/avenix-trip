## Colombo to Melbourne Direct Flights

## Colombo to Melbourne Time Difference

The time difference between Colombo and Melbourne changes during the year because Melbourne observes daylight saving time while Colombo does not.

Colombo follows Sri Lanka Standard Time (UTC+5:30) throughout the year.

Melbourne follows Australian Eastern Time:

- Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST): UTC+10.
- Australian Eastern Daylight Time (AEDT): UTC+11.

This means:

| Melbourne Time Period | Colombo Time Difference |
|---|---|
| Melbourne Standard Time | Melbourne is 4 hours 30 minutes ahead |
| Melbourne Daylight Saving Time | Melbourne is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead |

For example, when it is 10:00 AM in Colombo:

- During Melbourne standard time, it is 2:30 PM in Melbourne.
- During Melbourne daylight saving time, it is 3:30 PM in Melbourne.

Melbourne generally observes daylight saving time from October to April. The exact dates change each year.

This difference is useful when checking flight arrival times, arranging airport pickups or contacting family members in Australia.

Melbourne Airport (MEL), also known as Tullamarine Airport, is the main international airport serving Melbourne.

After landing, international passengers must complete the required Australian immigration and customs procedures.

The usual arrival process includes:

1. Following the signs to passport control.
2. Completing immigration checks.
3. Collecting checked baggage, if applicable.
4. Completing customs and biosecurity procedures.
5. Exiting into the arrivals area.

Australia has strict biosecurity rules. Certain food, plant and animal products must be declared, and some items may be restricted.

## Australian Visa and Entry Requirements

Passengers travelling from Colombo to Melbourne must meet Australia's entry requirements.

The required visa or travel permission depends on the traveller's nationality, passport, purpose of visit and personal circumstances.

Depending on eligibility, travellers may need a visitor visa or another appropriate Australian visa.

Before booking your journey:

- Check the visa requirements for your passport.
- Confirm that your passport is valid.
- Apply for the appropriate visa before travelling, if required.
- Review the conditions attached to your visa.
- Keep your travel and accommodation information accessible.

A confirmed flight ticket does not automatically provide permission to enter Australia.

Passengers who are only transiting through Australia should also check the applicable transit visa rules. Transit without a visa is available only to eligible nationalities and under specific conditions.

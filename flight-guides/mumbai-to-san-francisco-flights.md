# Mumbai to San Francisco Flights: Routes, Airlines and Travel Guide

Planning a journey from Mumbai to California? Finding the right **Mumbai to San Francisco flights** means comparing airlines, connecting airports, total travel time and ticket prices. The route connects Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) in Mumbai with San Francisco International Airport (SFO).

San Francisco is a popular destination for business travellers, international students, tourists and people visiting family in the United States. Since flight schedules and fares vary throughout the year, choosing the right connection can make a significant difference to your journey.

This guide covers available routes, popular airlines, stopover options, airport information and practical booking tips for your trip.

## How Long Is the Flight from Mumbai to San Francisco?

The flight time from Mumbai to San Francisco depends on the stopover airport, airline and connection schedule.

Some available one-stop routes have scheduled flying times starting at approximately 17 hours and 25 minutes, excluding the layover. Other options may take more than 23 hours of flying time before the connection wait is added.

For example, routes through Hong Kong and Tokyo can have shorter scheduled flying times than some European connections. However, the actual total journey depends on the time between flights.

When comparing flights, look at the complete journey duration shown on your ticket. A flight with a shorter time in the air may still take longer overall if it has an extended layover.

## Best Airlines for Mumbai to San Francisco Flights

Several international airlines offer connecting journeys between Mumbai and San Francisco. The best choice depends on your budget, preferred transit airport, baggage needs and departure time.

### 1. Cathay Pacific

Cathay Pacific offers connections through Hong Kong International Airport (HKG). This is one of the route options worth comparing when searching for Mumbai to San Francisco flights.

The Hong Kong connection can provide a single-stop itinerary. Before booking, check the layover duration and confirm that both flight segments are included in the same reservation.

### 2. Japan Airlines and ANA

Travellers can compare routes through Tokyo with Japan Airlines or All Nippon Airways (ANA), depending on available schedules and airline combinations.

Tokyo connections may use Haneda or Narita airport. Check the airport details carefully, especially if your itinerary requires changing between the two airports.

### 3. Singapore Airlines

Singapore Airlines offers one-stop connections through Singapore Changi Airport (SIN).

The airline confirms that it does not operate a nonstop Mumbai–San Francisco service. Its connecting itinerary through Singapore is an option for travellers who prefer to use a single major transit hub.

### 4. Emirates

Emirates connects Mumbai with San Francisco through Dubai International Airport (DXB).

When comparing this option, review the total connection time, arrival schedule and baggage conditions. A convenient departure time from Mumbai may help you plan the first part of your journey.

### 5. Qatar Airways

Qatar Airways offers connecting journeys through Hamad International Airport (DOH) in Doha.

Compare its available schedules with other one-stop routes. Check the time between arrival and departure in Doha before selecting your ticket.

### 6. Lufthansa, KLM and Other European Airlines

Travellers can also compare connections through European airports such as Frankfurt, Amsterdam, London, Paris and Copenhagen.

Depending on the date, possible airline options include Lufthansa, KLM, British Airways, Virgin Atlantic, Air France and Scandinavian Airlines.

Some itineraries may involve partner airlines or codeshare flights. Check which carrier operates each segment and whether your baggage is checked through to San Francisco.

## How to Find Cheap Flights from Mumbai to San Francisco

The cost of flights from Mumbai to San Francisco changes according to travel dates, seasonal demand, airline availability and booking conditions.

Use these tips to compare fares before making a reservation.

### Compare Flexible Travel Dates

If you can adjust your departure date, search several days before and after your preferred date. A small change in your schedule may reveal a different fare or a more convenient connection.

### Compare Different Stopover Cities

Do not limit your search to one airline or transit airport. Compare Hong Kong, Singapore, Doha, Dubai and European connections to see which offers the best combination of price and journey time.

### Check the Complete Fare

Some advertised fares may exclude checked baggage, seat selection or other optional services. Review the final price before paying.

### Consider the Layover Duration

A very short connection may leave little room for delays. A very long layover can make the journey tiring. Choose a connection that gives you enough time to transfer comfortably.

### Compare One-Way and Return Tickets

If your travel dates are fixed, compare round-trip fares with two separate one-way tickets. Make sure you understand the cancellation and change rules for each booking.

### Book Through a Protected Itinerary

Whenever possible, book connecting flights under one reservation. If a delay causes you to miss a protected connection, the airline's applicable rebooking conditions may help you continue your journey.

Separate tickets can carry additional risks because the second airline may not be responsible if you miss its flight due to a delay on the first booking.

## Departure Airport: Mumbai International Airport (BOM)

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport is Mumbai's main international airport. It handles domestic and international passenger services and has two main terminals.

Terminal 2 handles many international flights. However, your actual departure terminal depends on the airline and flight. Check your ticket and the airline's latest airport information before leaving.

For an international journey, allow sufficient time for check-in, baggage drop, security screening and immigration procedures.

Keep your passport, visa and flight confirmation accessible during airport formalities.

## Arrival Airport: San Francisco International Airport (SFO)

San Francisco International Airport is the main international airport serving San Francisco and the surrounding Bay Area.

After arriving from Mumbai, international passengers generally proceed through US immigration and customs.

If San Francisco is your final destination, you can continue to the city using airport transport options such as BART, taxis, rideshare services and airport shuttles.

If you have another domestic flight, allow enough time for immigration, baggage collection where required, customs clearance, security screening and any terminal transfer.

## Visa and Transit Requirements

Before booking your Mumbai to San Francisco flight, check the entry requirements for the United States and every transit country on your itinerary.

Indian passport holders generally need an appropriate US visa or another valid form of travel authorization for their circumstances. A confirmed airline ticket alone does not provide permission to enter the United States.

Transit requirements vary by country. Depending on your nationality, visa status, airport and itinerary, you may need a transit visa even if you do not plan to leave the airport.

If your itinerary includes a connection at another US airport before reaching San Francisco, you may need to complete US immigration and customs procedures at your first US point of entry.

Always confirm current requirements through official government immigration sources before departure.

## What to Check Before Booking

Before purchasing your ticket, review these important details:

- **Total journey time:** Include the connection wait, not just the flying time.
- **Stopover airport:** Confirm the airport and terminal for every flight.
- **Baggage allowance:** Check the rules for all operating airlines.
- **Transit requirements:** Verify whether you need permission to transit through the connecting country.
- **Ticket conditions:** Review cancellation, refund and date-change policies.
- **Connection protection:** Understand what happens if a delay causes you to miss another flight.
- **Arrival arrangements:** Consider your onward transport from San Francisco International Airport.

These checks can help you avoid unexpected expenses and connection problems.

## Manila to JFK Flight Time

The nonstop flight time from Manila to JFK is approximately 15 hours and 35 minutes.

The aircraft travels across the Pacific region before reaching the east coast of the United States. The actual route can vary because of weather, winds, air traffic and airspace restrictions.

The approximate distance between the two airports is 13,687 kilometres, or around 8,505 miles.

Passengers should remember that the scheduled flight duration is different from the total airport-to-airport travel experience. Check-in, security, immigration, boarding and baggage collection all add time to the journey.

### Why Does the Arrival Time Look Earlier Than the Departure Time?

Manila is ahead of New York by a substantial time difference.

Manila follows Philippine Time (UTC+8). New York follows Eastern Time, which changes between Eastern Standard Time (UTC-5) and Eastern Daylight Time (UTC-4).

During the US winter, Manila is 13 hours ahead of New York. During daylight saving time, Manila is 12 hours ahead.

This means a flight can leave Manila in the evening and arrive in New York on the same local calendar date, even though the aircraft has been travelling for more than 15 hours.

Always compare the local departure and arrival dates shown on your ticket.

## Manila and New York Time Difference

Manila and New York use different time zones.

Manila follows Philippine Time (PHT), UTC+8, throughout the year.

New York follows:

- Eastern Standard Time (EST), UTC-5, during winter
- Eastern Daylight Time (EDT), UTC-4, during daylight saving time

Therefore:

- During EST, Manila is 13 hours ahead of New York.
- During EDT, Manila is 12 hours ahead of New York.

For example, when it is 8:00 PM in Manila, it is 7:00 AM in New York during EST or 8:00 AM during EDT.

This difference is important when checking PR126's departure and arrival schedule.

## US Entry Requirements for Travellers from Manila

Before booking a flight from Manila to JFK, check the entry requirements for your passport nationality and travel purpose.

A flight ticket does not automatically provide permission to enter the United States.

Travellers may need a US visa, or they may qualify for travel under the Visa Waiver Program if they meet the eligibility conditions and obtain the required ESTA authorisation.

The Philippines is not currently a Visa Waiver Program country. Filipino passport holders travelling to the United States for tourism or business generally need the appropriate US visitor visa, subject to their individual circumstances.

Travellers holding passports from other countries should check the requirements for their own nationality.

For a temporary visit, the B-1/B-2 visitor visa may be relevant, depending on the purpose of travel.

Before departure:

- Check the visa requirements for your passport.
- Confirm that your visa is valid for your intended travel.
- Review the purpose and conditions of your admission.
- Carry supporting documents where appropriate.
- Check the latest US entry guidance.

## Bangkok to Vancouver Time Difference

The Bangkok to Vancouver time difference changes during the year because Vancouver observes daylight saving time while Bangkok does not.

Bangkok follows Indochina Time (ICT), which is UTC+7 throughout the year.

Vancouver follows Pacific Time:

- Pacific Standard Time (PST): UTC-8.
- Pacific Daylight Time (PDT): UTC-7.

This means:

| Period | Time Difference |
|---|---|
| Vancouver winter time | Bangkok is 15 hours ahead |
| Vancouver daylight saving time | Bangkok is 14 hours ahead |

For example, when it is 10:00 AM in Bangkok:

- During Vancouver winter time, it is 7:00 PM on the previous day in Vancouver.
- During Vancouver daylight saving time, it is 8:00 PM on the previous day in Vancouver.

The exact difference depends on the date.

Vancouver normally observes daylight saving time from March to early November. Bangkok does not change its clocks.

This time difference is important when checking flight arrival times, arranging airport pickups or contacting family members.

## Is a Direct Flight Better Than a Connecting Flight?

A direct flight can be more convenient because passengers do not need to change aircraft during the journey.

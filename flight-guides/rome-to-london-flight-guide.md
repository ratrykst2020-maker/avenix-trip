# Rome to London Flights: Flight Time, Airports and Travel Guide

Planning a trip between Italy and the United Kingdom? **Rome to London flights** offer a convenient way to travel between two of Europe's best-known capital cities. Whether you are travelling for a holiday, business meeting or a short city break, a direct flight can get you from Rome to London in around three hours.

Before booking, it helps to understand the available airports, typical flight duration, airline options and the differences between the two cities. This guide covers everything you should know before arranging your journey.

## Rome to London Flights: Quick Overview

Here are some useful details to consider when planning your flight:

| Travel detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Departure city | Rome, Italy |
| Arrival city | London, United Kingdom |
| Main Rome airport | Rome Fiumicino (FCO) |
| Main London airport | London Heathrow (LHR) |
| Approximate direct flight time | 2 hours 50 minutes |
| Approximate airport distance | 1,444 km |
| Flight options | Direct and connecting flights |
| Main direct airlines on FCO–LHR | British Airways and ITA Airways |

Flight schedules, journey times and available airlines can change. Always check the details for your selected travel date before booking.

## Flights from Rome to London: Which Airlines Operate This Route?

Several airlines operate flights between Rome and London. The airlines and airports available depend on your departure date, destination airport and preferred travel time.

For the Rome Fiumicino to London Heathrow route, British Airways and ITA Airways operate direct services. Other flight options may connect Rome with London through different airports.

When comparing flights from Rome to London, consider:

- Whether the flight is direct or includes a connection.
- Which London airport the aircraft arrives at.
- Whether checked baggage is included.
- Departure and arrival times.
- Airport transfer costs after landing.
- Ticket change and cancellation conditions.

A cheaper ticket may not always be the most convenient option. For example, arriving at an airport farther from central London could mean additional transport time and expense.

## Flight Time Rome to London: How Long Does It Take?

The **flight time Rome to London** depends on the airports, airline, weather and flight route.

A direct flight from Rome Fiumicino Airport (FCO) to London Heathrow Airport (LHR) takes approximately **2 hours and 50 minutes**. Some schedules may show a slightly different duration.

Connecting flights take longer because passengers must change aircraft or wait at another airport.

Remember that the scheduled flight duration does not include the time needed for airport check-in, security, boarding, baggage collection or travel to and from the airports.

### How Long Is the Flight from London to Rome?

The return journey from London to Rome also takes approximately 2 hours and 30 minutes to 2 hours and 50 minutes on many direct services, depending on the airports and flight schedule.

There is a one-hour time difference between London and Rome. Rome uses Central European Time, while London uses UK time. This difference can make the arrival time on your ticket look shorter or longer than the actual time spent in the air.

For an accurate journey estimate, compare the departure and arrival times on the airline's booking page and check the local time zone for each airport.

## Which Airports Can You Use?

Choosing the right airport can make your journey more convenient.

### Airports in Rome

**1. Leonardo da Vinci–Fiumicino Airport (FCO)**

Fiumicino is Rome's main international airport. It handles many international and European flights and is a major departure point for services to London.

**2. Ciampino Airport (CIA)**

Ciampino is another airport serving Rome. It is used by some European airlines, including low-cost operators. Check whether your selected London service departs from this airport before booking.

### Airports in London

London has several airports. Depending on the airline and flight, you may arrive at:

- Heathrow Airport (LHR)
- Gatwick Airport (LGW)
- Stansted Airport (STN)
- Luton Airport (LTN)
- London City Airport (LCY)

Do not choose a flight based on ticket price alone. Check the distance between the arrival airport and your hotel or final destination. Ground transportation can affect the overall cost and travel time.

## How to Choose the Best Rome to London Flight

The best flight depends on your schedule, budget and travel preferences.

**Choose a direct flight if you want to save time.** Direct services avoid the need to change aircraft and are generally more convenient for short trips.

**Compare different London airports.** A ticket arriving at Heathrow may suit someone staying in central or west London, while another airport could be more convenient for a different destination.

**Check baggage rules.** Some fares include only a small personal item. Additional cabin or checked baggage may increase the final price.

**Compare the full journey cost.** Include airport transfers, baggage fees and any additional charges when comparing ticket prices.

**Book after checking the travel conditions.** Review the airline's rules for changes, cancellations and delays before completing your purchase.

## How Big Is Rome Compared to London?

Rome and London are both large European capitals, but their geographical size depends on which administrative boundaries are being compared.

Rome's municipal area covers approximately **1,287 square kilometres**. Greater London covers approximately **1,572 square kilometres**.

Using these administrative figures, Greater London is around 285 square kilometres larger than Rome's municipality.

However, the comparison needs some context. Greater London includes 32 boroughs and the City of London, while Rome's municipal area is governed under a different administrative structure. Metropolitan areas extend beyond these boundaries and produce different figures.

The two cities also have different urban layouts. Rome includes extensive green areas, historic districts and less densely built-up areas. London contains a large network of boroughs, neighbourhoods, parks and transport connections.

For travellers, the practical difference is that both cities are large enough to require planning between airports, accommodation and major attractions.

## What to Know Before Travelling from Rome to London

Before leaving Rome, make sure you have checked the following:

- **Local currency:** The United Kingdom uses the British pound, while Italy uses the euro.

Travellers should check official government guidance for entry requirements before purchasing a non-refundable ticket.

## Is Flying from Rome to London Worth It?

For most travellers making this journey, flying is a practical option, especially when time is limited. Direct flights take around three hours and connect Rome with several London airports.

However, the total journey includes airport transfers, check-in and waiting time. If you are travelling between city centres, compare the full door-to-door journey rather than looking only at the time spent in the air.

For a short holiday or business trip, a direct flight can be a convenient choice. Selecting the right airport and checking baggage conditions can make the journey easier.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Rome to London Flights

### 1. How long is the flight from Rome to London?

A direct flight from Rome Fiumicino to London Heathrow takes approximately 2 hours and 50 minutes. Other routes and airport combinations may have different flight times.

### 2. How long is the flight from London to Rome?

A direct London-to-Rome flight generally takes around 2 hours and 30 minutes to 2 hours and 50 minutes, depending on the airports and schedule.

### 3. Are there direct flights from Rome to London?

Yes. Direct services operate between Rome Fiumicino and London Heathrow. Other direct routes may also be available depending on the airline and travel date.

### 4. Which airlines fly from Rome to London?

British Airways and ITA Airways operate direct flights between Rome Fiumicino and London Heathrow. Other airlines may offer services to different London airports.

### 5. How far is Rome from London by air?

The air distance between Rome Fiumicino and London Heathrow is approximately 1,444 kilometres. The exact distance varies depending on the airports used.

### 6. How big is Rome compared to London?

Rome's municipal area is approximately 1,287 square kilometres, while Greater London covers around 1,572 square kilometres. Greater London is larger by area using these administrative boundaries.

### 7. Is London one hour behind Rome?

Yes. London is normally one hour behind Rome. Both cities observe daylight saving time, with the same one-hour difference during their respective summer periods.

### 8. Which London airport is best for arriving from Rome?

It depends on where you are staying. Heathrow may be convenient for many central and western London destinations, while Gatwick, Stansted and Luton may suit travellers staying in other areas.

### 9. Should I book a direct or connecting flight?

A direct flight is generally more convenient when saving time is your priority. A connecting flight may suit you if its schedule or overall price better matches your plans.

# Dhaka to Tokyo Flights: Routes, Ticket Prices and Travel Guide

Planning a trip from Bangladesh to Japan? Finding the right **Dhaka to Tokyo flights** involves comparing airlines, departure schedules, connecting airports and ticket prices. Whether you are travelling for tourism, business, education or visiting family, choosing a suitable flight can make your journey more comfortable.

Travellers departing from Dhaka can compare nonstop services to Tokyo with connecting flights through Singapore, Hong Kong, Bangkok, Guangzhou and other international hubs.

One popular option is **Dhaka to Tokyo via Singapore**, operated by Singapore Airlines. This route connects Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport in Dhaka with Singapore Changi Airport before continuing to Tokyo.

This guide explains the available routes, airline options, approximate travel times, ticket prices and important information to check before booking.

## Dhaka to Tokyo Flight: Route Overview

The journey starts at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC) and arrives at either Narita International Airport (NRT) or Haneda Airport (HND), depending on the airline and itinerary.

| Route detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Departure airport | Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC) |
| Departure city | Dhaka, Bangladesh |
| Destination | Tokyo, Japan |
| Arrival airports | Narita (NRT) or Haneda (HND) |
| Approximate direct distance | 4,897 km |
| Direct flight option | Biman Bangladesh Airlines on selected days |
| Connecting options | Singapore, Hong Kong, Bangkok, Guangzhou and others |
| Travel time | Depends on route and layover |

The direct flight option can save time, while a connecting flight may offer more departure choices or a different fare.

## Dhaka to Tokyo via Singapore

Singapore is a popular transit point for travellers flying from Bangladesh to Japan. Singapore Airlines operates a one-stop connection between Dhaka and Tokyo through Singapore Changi Airport (SIN).

The journey generally consists of two flight segments:

| Flight segment | Approximate flight time |
|---|---|
| Dhaka (DAC) to Singapore (SIN) | 4 hours 5 minutes |
| Singapore (SIN) to Tokyo | 7 hours 5 minutes |
| Total flying time | Around 11 hours 10 minutes |
| Additional time | Singapore layover |

The actual journey duration depends on the connecting schedule and whether the Tokyo flight arrives at Haneda or Narita.

### Why Choose Singapore Airlines?

Singapore Airlines offers a connecting itinerary that allows passengers to travel from Dhaka to Tokyo through one major international hub.

Some features of this route include:

- One-stop connection through Singapore Changi Airport.
- Economy and Business Class options on the Dhaka–Singapore sector.
- Different cabin options on the Singapore–Tokyo sector, depending on the aircraft and flight.
- Meals and in-flight entertainment on the journey.
- Baggage through-checking may be available when both sectors are on an eligible single booking.

Check the final ticket for the operating airline, aircraft, baggage allowance and cabin details.

### What to Expect at Singapore Changi Airport

Singapore Changi Airport is a major international transit hub. Passengers connecting there can find restaurants, shopping areas, rest spaces and airport facilities.

If you have a short connection, remain aware of your next departure gate and boarding time. For a longer layover, you may have more time to explore the airport.

Do not assume that every layover allows you to enter Singapore. Leaving the airport depends on your eligibility and the applicable entry requirements.

## Direct Flights from Dhaka to Tokyo

Travellers who prefer to avoid a stopover can check Biman Bangladesh Airlines.

Biman operates nonstop services between Dhaka and Tokyo Narita on selected days. The schedule can change, so confirm the operating date and flight availability before booking.

A nonstop service can be useful if you want to reduce the time spent in transit and avoid changing aircraft.

However, nonstop flights may not operate every day. If your preferred date does not match the schedule, a connecting itinerary may be a better option.

## Dhaka to Tokyo Air Ticket Price

The **Dhaka to Tokyo air ticket price** changes depending on the airline, booking date, travel season, cabin class, baggage allowance and seat availability.

There is no single fixed price for this route. A fare displayed today may change when you search again or select different travel dates.

For a realistic comparison, check the following:

| Ticket option | What affects the price |
|---|---|
| One-way Economy | Airline, date and available seats |
| Return Economy | Return date and seasonal demand |
| Business Class | Cabin availability and fare conditions |
| Nonstop flight | Operating schedule and seat availability |
| One-stop flight | Airline, transit city and layover duration |

Online fare listings can display prices in USD, BDT, JPY or other currencies. Always check the final amount in your preferred currency before payment.

### How to Find a Cheaper Air Ticket

**Compare multiple dates:** Search a few dates before and after your planned departure. Flexible dates may reveal different fares.

**Compare direct and connecting flights:** A nonstop flight can save time, but a connecting itinerary may offer a different price.

**Check return fares:** If you know your return date, compare round-trip prices with separate one-way tickets.

**Review baggage allowances:** A cheaper fare may include less checked baggage. Check the ticket conditions before booking.

**Book with enough time:** Booking early can give you more options, although prices are never guaranteed to decrease as departure approaches.

**Check the final payment amount:** Include taxes, service charges, baggage fees and any other required costs when comparing offers.

## Narita or Haneda: Which Tokyo Airport Should You Choose?

Tokyo has two major international airports. Your arrival airport can affect your onward journey.

### Narita International Airport (NRT)

Narita International Airport is located outside central Tokyo. It handles many international services and offers rail and bus connections to different parts of the city.

It may be suitable if your flight arrives there and your accommodation or onward destination is accessible by Narita Express, other railway services or airport buses.

### Haneda Airport (HND)

Haneda Airport is closer to central Tokyo and handles both domestic and international flights.

Travellers staying in central Tokyo may find Haneda convenient because of its access to local railways and airport transport.

Before booking, compare the airfare and the ground transportation cost from the airport to your accommodation.

## Important Travel Information for Bangladeshi Passengers

Before travelling from Dhaka to Tokyo, prepare your travel documents and check the latest entry rules.

### Japan Visa Requirements

Bangladeshi passport holders generally need a visa to visit Japan for tourism or other short-term purposes, unless they qualify for a specific exemption.

Visa requirements depend on the purpose of travel and the applicant's circumstances. Check the Embassy of Japan in Bangladesh or the official Japanese visa information before applying.

### Transit Requirements

If your flight connects through Singapore or another country, check the transit rules for that airport.

Some passengers can remain in the international transit area without entering the country, while others may need additional permission depending on their itinerary and circumstances.

### Baggage and Airport Check-in

Check the baggage allowance for every flight segment. If you are travelling with one booking, the airline may be able to check your baggage through to Tokyo, subject to its rules and the itinerary.

Arrive at Dhaka airport early enough to complete international check-in, immigration and security procedures.

## What to Check Before Booking

Before confirming your Dhaka to Tokyo flight, review these details:

- **Arrival airport:** Confirm whether your flight lands at Narita or Haneda.
- **Total journey time:** Include the stopover duration.
- **Transit requirements:** Check the rules for Singapore or any other connecting country.
- **Baggage allowance:** Confirm the allowance shown on your ticket.
- **Ticket type:** Review cancellation, refund and date-change conditions.
- **Connection protection:** Check whether all flight segments are covered by one booking.
- **Arrival transport:** Plan how you will travel from the Tokyo airport to your accommodation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long is the flight from Dhaka to Tokyo?

The actual flying time depends on whether you take a nonstop or connecting service. A nonstop journey takes approximately 6 hours and 50 minutes. Connecting routes can take longer, especially when the layover is included.

### Is there a direct flight from Dhaka to Tokyo?

Yes. Biman Bangladesh Airlines operates nonstop flights between Dhaka and Tokyo Narita on selected days. Check the latest schedule for your travel date.

### How can I travel from Dhaka to Tokyo via Singapore?

You can book a one-stop itinerary with Singapore Airlines. The journey connects Dhaka to Singapore Changi Airport and then continues to Tokyo.

### What is the Dhaka to Tokyo air ticket price?

Ticket prices vary by airline, travel date, cabin class and seat availability. Compare one-way and return fares and check the final price, including taxes and baggage.

### Which airlines fly from Dhaka to Tokyo?

Airline options include Biman Bangladesh Airlines, Singapore Airlines, Cathay Pacific, China Southern, China Eastern, Thai Airways and other carriers offering connecting services.

### Is Singapore Airlines a good option for Dhaka to Tokyo?

Singapore Airlines is an option for travellers who prefer a one-stop connection through Singapore. Compare the total journey time, fare, baggage allowance and layover before booking.

### Does Singapore Airlines provide baggage through-checking?

Baggage is typically checked through to Tokyo when eligible flight segments are booked under one itinerary and the connection meets the airline's conditions. Confirm the baggage destination printed on your baggage tag at check-in.

### Which Tokyo airport is better, Narita or Haneda?

Haneda is closer to central Tokyo, while Narita is farther away but has rail and bus connections to the city. Choose based on your flight price, arrival time and accommodation location.

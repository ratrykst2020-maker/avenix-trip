# Dhaka to Sydney: Distance, Flight Time and Ticket Price

Planning a trip from Bangladesh to Australia? Before booking your journey, it helps to understand the **Dhaka to Sydney distance**, total flight time and available ticket prices. Whether you are travelling for education, business, tourism or visiting family, comparing airlines and connecting routes can help you find a suitable flight.

The journey connects Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC) in Dhaka with Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport (SYD) in Australia. Several international airlines offer connecting services, with popular stopover cities including Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok, Hong Kong and Doha.

This guide covers the distance, journey duration, airline options, ticket prices and practical booking information.

## Dhaka to Sydney Distance

The approximate straight-line distance between Dhaka and Sydney is **9,093 kilometres (5,650 miles)**.

However, the actual distance travelled by an aircraft can be longer because most available itineraries include a stopover. The route depends on the connecting airport, airline and flight path.

| Route detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Departure city | Dhaka, Bangladesh |
| Arrival city | Sydney, Australia |
| Departure airport | Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC) |
| Arrival airport | Sydney Airport (SYD) |
| Approximate direct distance | 9,093 km (5,650 miles) |
| Direct flights | No regular nonstop service currently listed |
| Common stopovers | Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok, Hong Kong and Doha |

The distance is a geographical estimate, not the exact distance of every connecting itinerary.

### What Affects the Ticket Price?

Several factors can change the price of flights from Dhaka to Sydney:

**Travel season:** Prices may increase during Australian summer holidays, Christmas, New Year and busy student travel periods.

**Departure date:** Weekend and peak-season departures may cost more than flights on less popular dates.

**Airline:** Different carriers have different fare structures, baggage allowances and booking conditions.

**Stopover duration:** Some connecting itineraries may be cheaper than others, but longer layovers can increase total travel time.

**Cabin class:** Economy, Premium Economy, Business and First Class have different prices and services.

### How to Find Cheaper Flights

- Compare several departure and return dates.
- Search both one-way and return tickets.
- Compare different stopover cities.
- Check the baggage allowance before choosing the lowest fare.
- Review the final price, including taxes and service fees.
- Compare cancellation and date-change conditions.
- Consider booking connecting flights under one reservation for better connection protection.

## Best Airlines for Dhaka to Sydney Flights

Several international airlines provide connecting services between Dhaka and Sydney.

### 1. Singapore Airlines

Singapore Airlines offers a one-stop journey through Singapore Changi Airport (SIN). Passengers travel from Dhaka to Singapore before continuing to Sydney.

The airline operates daily connecting services on this route. Its official route information lists approximately 11 hours and 45 minutes to more than 13 hours, depending on the connection.

This is an option worth comparing if you prefer to transit through Singapore.

### 2. Malaysia Airlines

Malaysia Airlines offers connecting flights through Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL).

The route may suit travellers who want to connect through Malaysia. Check the available departure times, total journey duration and baggage conditions.

### 3. Thai Airways

Thai Airways offers connections through Bangkok. Travellers can compare its schedules and fares with other Asian airlines.

### 4. Emirates

Emirates connects Dhaka and Sydney through Dubai International Airport (DXB).

This route may involve a longer journey than some Asian connections, depending on the schedule. Compare the total travel time before booking.

### 5. Qatar Airways

Qatar Airways offers connecting flights through Hamad International Airport (DOH) in Doha.

Check the layover duration and onward departure schedule to see whether this route suits your travel plans.

### 6. Cathay Pacific

Cathay Pacific offers connections through Hong Kong International Airport (HKG). This is another option for travellers comparing one-stop journeys from Dhaka to Australia.

### 7. China Southern Airlines

China Southern offers connecting services through Guangzhou. Compare the flight schedule, connection time and ticket conditions for your preferred dates.

## Departure Airport: Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC)

Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport is the main international airport serving Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Passengers travelling to Sydney should check their airline's terminal, check-in counter and baggage rules before leaving for the airport.

For international travel, allow enough time for check-in, baggage drop, immigration and security screening.

Keep your passport, visa, ticket confirmation and other required travel documents accessible.

## Arrival Airport: Sydney Airport (SYD)

Sydney Airport, also known as Kingsford Smith Airport, is the main international airport serving Sydney.

After arriving from Dhaka, international passengers generally proceed through Australian immigration, baggage collection and customs.

The airport offers several transport options for reaching the city, including:

- Airport train services
- Public buses
- Taxis
- Rideshare services
- Private airport transfers

If you are travelling to another Australian city after landing, allow enough time for immigration, baggage collection and domestic flight check-in.

## Visa and Transit Requirements

Bangladeshi passport holders generally need an appropriate Australian visa before travelling to Sydney.

The correct visa depends on the purpose of travel. Tourist visitors, students and people travelling for business may need different visa categories.

Check the Australian Department of Home Affairs website for current eligibility, application requirements and processing information.

You should also check transit requirements for your connecting country. Whether you need a transit visa depends on your passport, visa status, airport and itinerary.

If you book separate tickets, you may need to collect your baggage and check in again during the connection. This can also affect whether you need permission to enter the transit country.

## What to Check Before Booking

Before purchasing your Dhaka to Sydney ticket, review these details:

- **Flight duration:** Check the total journey time, including the layover.
- **Stopover airport:** Confirm the connecting airport and terminal.
- **Baggage allowance:** Check the rules for every airline on your booking.
- **Visa requirements:** Confirm Australian entry and transit requirements.
- **Ticket conditions:** Review refund, cancellation and date-change rules.
- **Connection protection:** Check whether all flight segments are on one reservation.
- **Arrival transport:** Plan how you will travel from Sydney Airport to your destination.

A careful comparison can help you avoid unexpected costs and connection problems.

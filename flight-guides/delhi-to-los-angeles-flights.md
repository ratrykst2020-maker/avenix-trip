# Delhi to Los Angeles Flights: Routes, Airlines and Travel Guide

Planning a trip from India to California? Finding suitable **Delhi to Los Angeles flights** requires comparing airlines, stopover cities, total journey times and ticket prices. The route connects Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) in New Delhi with Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), one of the busiest airports on the US West Coast.

There are several ways to make this journey, including connections through Tokyo, Hong Kong, Singapore, Doha, Dubai and European cities. Your best option will depend on your travel date, budget, preferred airline and how long you are comfortable spending in transit.

This guide explains what to expect when booking flights from Delhi to Los Angeles and how to choose a suitable connection.

## Delhi to Los Angeles Flight: Route Overview

The journey begins at Indira Gandhi International Airport in New Delhi and ends at Los Angeles International Airport in California.

Here are the main route details:

| Route detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Departure airport | Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) |
| Arrival airport | Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) |
| Departure city | New Delhi, India |
| Arrival city | Los Angeles, California, USA |
| Approximate direct distance | 12,891 km (8,010 miles) |
| Direct flights | No regular nonstop service currently listed |
| Typical connections | One or more stopovers |
| Common transit cities | Tokyo, Hong Kong, Singapore, Doha, Dubai and European hubs |

There are currently no nonstop flights listed between Delhi and Los Angeles. Travellers generally need to change planes at an intermediate airport. The available routes and schedules can change, so check the itinerary for your exact travel date before booking.

## How Long Is the Flight from Delhi to Los Angeles?

The total journey time depends on the airline, connecting airport and length of the layover.

Some one-stop itineraries have flight times of approximately 17 hours and 30 minutes to 21 hours and 20 minutes, excluding the time spent waiting between flights. Other connections can take considerably longer.

For example, routes through Tokyo or Hong Kong may offer relatively short scheduled flying times. A connection through Doha, Dubai, London or Frankfurt may have a different total journey time depending on the available flights.

Remember that the advertised flight duration and the complete travel time are different. Always check both before choosing a ticket.

## Best Airlines for Flights from Delhi to Los Angeles

Several international airlines offer connecting services between Delhi and Los Angeles. The most suitable airline depends on your preferred stopover, ticket price, baggage allowance and departure time.

### 1. Japan Airlines and ANA

Travelling through Tokyo is one option worth comparing. Depending on the itinerary, passengers may connect through Tokyo Haneda or Narita before continuing to Los Angeles.

Japan Airlines and All Nippon Airways (ANA) operate services on relevant parts of these routes. Check the airport combination carefully because changing between Tokyo airports can require additional ground transportation and time.

### 2. Cathay Pacific

Cathay Pacific offers connections through Hong Kong International Airport. This can be a convenient option for travellers who prefer a single stop in Asia.

Before booking, compare the layover duration and confirm whether your entire journey is issued on one ticket.

### 3. Singapore Airlines

Singapore Airlines offers one-stop journeys through Singapore Changi Airport. Its Delhi-to-Los Angeles itinerary connects through Singapore rather than operating a nonstop service.

This option may suit passengers who prefer to travel through a major international hub. Check the available connection and total travel time for your date.

### 4. Emirates

Emirates connects Delhi and Los Angeles through Dubai International Airport. Travellers can compare its departure times, connection duration and baggage conditions with other airlines.

### 5. Qatar Airways

Qatar Airways offers connecting services through Hamad International Airport in Doha. Depending on the schedule, this can be another option for travelling from India to Southern California.

### 6. European Airlines

Passengers can also compare routes through European hubs such as Amsterdam, Frankfurt, Helsinki and London.

Airlines and combinations may include KLM, Lufthansa, Finnair, British Airways and Virgin Atlantic. Some itineraries involve partner airlines, so check which airline operates each individual flight.

## How to Find Cheap Flights from Delhi to Los Angeles

The cost of flights from Delhi to Los Angeles can vary significantly. Travel dates, demand, booking time, airline and baggage conditions all affect the final fare.

Use these practical tips to compare prices:

### Compare Different Travel Dates

If your schedule is flexible, search several departure dates. A flight departing a day earlier or later may have a different fare or a shorter connection.

### Compare One-Stop Itineraries

A one-stop journey may save time compared with some itineraries involving two connections. However, a longer layover can sometimes reduce the ticket price. Compare the complete journey rather than looking only at the first flight.

### Check the Total Ticket Cost

A low advertised fare may not include every service you need. Review checked baggage, seat selection, meal conditions, change fees and cancellation rules before purchasing.

### Search Multiple Airlines

Compare the available options from Emirates, Qatar Airways, Cathay Pacific, Singapore Airlines, Japan Airlines and other carriers. Check whether a single airline operates the whole journey or whether separate airlines are involved.

### Book a Protected Connection

Whenever possible, choose a single booking that covers all flight segments. If you book separate tickets, a delay on the first flight may cause you to miss the next one without the same protection.

## Which Airport Will You Use?

### Departure: Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL)

Indira Gandhi International Airport serves New Delhi and the surrounding National Capital Region. It is a major international gateway with flights to destinations across Asia, Europe, the Middle East and North America.

Before departure, confirm your terminal, check-in counter and baggage rules with your airline. Allow enough time for international check-in, security screening and immigration.

### Arrival: Los Angeles International Airport (LAX)

Los Angeles International Airport is the main international gateway for travellers visiting Los Angeles and much of Southern California.

After landing, international passengers generally proceed through US immigration and customs. The airport has multiple terminals, and your arrival terminal depends on the airline and flight.

If you have a connecting domestic flight, allow enough time for immigration, baggage collection where required, customs clearance and any terminal transfer.

## Important Visa and Transit Information

A flight ticket does not automatically give you permission to enter or transit through another country.

Indian passport holders travelling to the United States generally need the appropriate US visa or other valid travel authorization for their circumstances. Check the current US entry requirements before booking.

You should also check the transit rules for your stopover country. Requirements can depend on your nationality, visa status, airport, itinerary and whether you need to leave the international transit area.

Pay particular attention to connections through the United States. If your itinerary includes a US airport before Los Angeles, you may need to complete US immigration procedures at that first US point of entry.

For the latest requirements, consult the official government immigration websites of the countries involved.

## What to Check Before Booking

Before confirming your Delhi to Los Angeles flight, review these details:

- **Total journey time:** Include the layover, not just the time in the air.
- **Stopover airport:** Confirm the airport and terminal for every flight segment.
- **Baggage allowance:** Check the rules for all airlines on the booking.
- **Transit requirements:** Verify whether you need a transit visa.
- **Ticket protection:** Understand what happens if a connecting flight is delayed.
- **Arrival time:** Consider local transport and accommodation arrangements in Los Angeles.
- **Fare conditions:** Review refund, cancellation and date-change policies.

A little preparation can help you avoid unexpected costs and stressful airport transfers.
